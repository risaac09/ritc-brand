How this page is made

Reach, views, interactions, profile visits and active times are copied by hand from Instagram Insights on the club account. Insights needs the club login, so nobody outside the club can pull them. Followers, likes, comments and reel plays are public counts from the profile. Partner posts are posts on the club's grid, collabs included, that tag or are posted by On (@on) or Run Newport (@runnewport).

Once a month, before the first-Monday committee meeting, the social media chair updates the files in report/data/ and adds a row to the log below. This page is plain HTML in the club's ritc-brand repository, so every change is a commit: full change history.
